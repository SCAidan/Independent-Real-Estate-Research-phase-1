WHY AN APPRAISAL?

A clearer basis
for your next decision.

An independent opinion of property value can support more than a purchase or a mortgage.

01

Buying & borrowing

Home purchases

Understand a property’s market value before making an offer.

Mortgages, refinancing & equity loans

Appraisals support primary and secondary mortgage lending, refinancing, and home equity borrowing.

Private mortgage insurance review

A valuation may support a request to review PMI. Confirm your lender’s requirements before ordering.

02

Life’s transitions

Divorce settlement

An independent valuation can inform discussions about a sale or one party buying out the other.

Estate planning & liquidation

Establish a property value to support estate administration and planning.

Employee relocation

Coordinate an inspection and share relevant improvements or neighborhood sales for consideration.

03

Selling & improving

Setting a selling price

Use an independent appraisal to inform your asking price, whether selling privately or through an agent.

Considering improvements

Understand how proposed updates may affect value. A project’s cost and its contribution to market value can differ.
