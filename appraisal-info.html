THE APPRAISAL PROCESS

From property details
to an informed opinion.

An appraisal is an independent opinion of real estate value, developed through research and analysis.

01

Property inspection

The appraiser reviews the location, layout, size, features, condition, and visible issues that may affect value. A sketch may document dimensions and layout.

02

Research & valuation approaches

Sales comparison

Recent comparable sales provide a basis for analysis, with adjustments for relevant differences such as size, features, condition, and location.

Cost approach

Construction costs and related factors help assess the cost of a similar property.

Income approach

For suitable income-producing properties, rental income can contribute to the valuation analysis.

03

Reconciliation & reporting

The appraiser weighs the available evidence and applicable approaches to reach a value opinion. An appraised value may differ from the eventual sale price.
