APPRAISER ETHICS

Independent work.
Professional standards.

An objective value opinion starts with professional responsibility.

IRER

Research guided by the assignment.
Results guided by the evidence.

Objectivity

Assignments are not accepted on the condition that they reach a predetermined value or favor a desired result.

Independent fees

Fees are not based on a property’s value or contingent on a loan closing.

Confidentiality

Client information and reports are handled with discretion. When the lender orders an appraisal, report requests should generally go through that lender.

Professional responsibility

Competence, education, accuracy, and appropriate recordkeeping support the work. The original site identifies the Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice (USPAP) as a foundation for its ethical commitments.
